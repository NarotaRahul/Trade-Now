Position Trading

Position trading is a long-term trading strategy where traders hold positions for weeks, months, or even years, aiming to profit from large price trends over time.

Key Features

  • Based on fundamental and long-term technical analysis.
  • Focuses on major market trends, not short-term moves.
  • Low trade frequency and lower transaction costs.

Advantages

  • Minimal time commitment for monitoring trades.
  • Potential for significant returns on long-term moves.
  • Reduces emotional trading and market noise.

Disadvantages

  • Capital is locked in for extended periods.
  • Requires strong patience and discipline.
  • Risk of large drawdowns during trend reversals.

Common Instruments

  • Index Funds & ETFs
  • Blue-Chip Stocks
  • Commodities like Gold & Oil
  • Major Forex Pairs

Ideal For

Position trading suits investors with a long-term outlook who prefer stability and are less concerned with short-term volatility.
